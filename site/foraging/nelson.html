What you can pick in Nelson

The most mapped plants around Nelson, with how many spots are on the map, when they’re usually ready and and how to tell they’re ready.

54

Blackberry

Rubus fruticosus

When: Ripe from mid summer into early autumn.

Tip: Pick when berries are fully black, plump, and come away easily without resistance.

18

Elderberry

Sambucus nigra

When: Flowers in late spring; berries ripen in late summer to early autumn.

Tip: Harvest when clusters are fully dark purple-black and all berries in the bunch are ripe.

17

Bay Leaf

Laurus nobilis

When: Leaves can be picked all year round.

Tip: They are used whole, dried, or occasionally ground to flavour dishes.

13

Fig

Ficus carica

When: Ripe from late summer into autumn.

Tip: Pick when fruit hangs slightly limp, feels soft, and the skin may show slight splitting or drooping.

12

Peach

Prunus persica

When: Ripe from early to late summer, depending on the variety.

Tip: Harvest when fruit is fully coloured, fragrant, and slightly soft around the stem.

11

Fennel

Foeniculum vulgare

When: Leaves and fronds can be picked from spring to autumn; flowers in summer and seeds ripen in late summer to autumn.

Tip: Big white bulb with green stalks and feathery leaves.

10

Feijoa

Acca sellowiana

When: The main season generally occurs in autumn in temperate regions.

Tip: Pick when fruits drop naturally or come away with a gentle shake of the tree.

9

Plum

Prunus domestica

When: Ripe from mid summer into early autumn, depending on the variety.

Tip: Harvest when fruit is fully coloured, fragrant, and slightly soft near the stem.

7

Hazelnut

Corylus americana

When: Ripe in late summer to early autumn, when the husks brown and nuts drop.

Tip: Hazelnuts are ready to harvest in late summer to early autumn when the husks turn from green to yellow-brown and the nuts begin dropping naturally from the tree.

7

Apple

Malus domestica

When: Ripe from late summer through autumn, depending on the variety and climate.

Tip: Ripe when fruit is fully coloured, comes off easily with a gentle twist, and seeds are brown.

6

Blueberry

Vaccinium corymbosum

When: Ripe from late spring through summer, earlier in warm areas.

Tip: Pick only when berries are fully blue with a matte bloom and detach easily.

5

Grape

Vitis vinifera

When: Ripe from late summer to autumn, depending on the variety.

Tip: Harvest when berries are fully coloured, sweet, and easily detach from the stem.

Where to look

The most mapped suburbs right now are Stoke (76), Maitai (50), Hira (14), Richmond (12), The Wood (8), Enner Glynn (7), Appleby (7) and Tāhunanui (6). Many more spots are scattered across the rest of Nelson, so check the map for what’s near you.

There are also 64 public drinking water taps and fountains on the map, handy for a long walk or ride. And 1 community gardens, many of which welcome visitors and volunteers.

Forage responsibly

  • Ask before picking from trees on private land, even if branches hang over the footpath.
  • Check your local council’s rules for parks and reserves.
  • Take only what you need and leave plenty for others and for wildlife.
  • Only eat what you can identify with certainty, and wash what you pick, especially near busy roads.

What you can pick in the Gold Coast

The most mapped plants around Gold Coast, with how many spots are on the map, when they’re usually ready and and how to tell they’re ready.

91

Mango

Mangifera indica

When: Ripe from late spring to late summer, starting earlier in the tropics than in subtropical areas.

Tip: Harvest when the fruit reaches full size and starts to show colour change or a slight yellow blush, depending on variety.

7

Passionfruit

Passiflora edulis

When: Main crop in summer to autumn, often a smaller crop in spring.

Tip: Pick when fruit changes from green to deep purple or yellow and often falls from the vine naturally.

7

Guava

Psidium guajava

When: Ripe from late summer into winter, with some fruit at other times in the tropics.

Tip: Pick when fruit gives slightly to pressure and emits a strong sweet fragrance.

5

Macadamia

Macadamia integrifolia

When: Nuts fall mainly from late summer to winter.

Tip: Harvest when nuts drop naturally or husks split open on the tree.

5

Finger Lime

Citrus australasica

When: Ripe from late summer to winter, varying between trees.

Tip: Finger limes are ripe when they feel slightly soft to the touch, have a subtle sheen on the skin, emit a pleasant citrus aroma, and detach easily from the tree.

5

Lemon

Citrus limon

When: Fruits most of the year, with the main crop in winter to spring.

Tip: A lemon is ripe when it has a bright yellow color, feels firm with a slight give, emits a strong citrus aroma, and is heavy for its size.

4

Mandarin

Citrus reticulata

When: Ripe in late autumn through winter, depending on the variety.

Tip: Pick when fruit is fully orange, fragrant, and slightly soft with a heavy feel.

4

Persimmon

Diospyros kaki

When: Ripe in autumn, usually after the leaves start colouring and dropping.

Tip: Pick when fruit is fully coloured and starting to soften, but still holds its shape—unless it’s a astringent type, which should be left until very soft.

4

Loquat

Eriobotrya japonica

When: Ripe in late winter to spring, one of the first fruits of the year.

Tip: Pick when fruit is fully yellow-orange, fragrant, and slightly soft to the touch.

3

Dragonfruit

Selenicereus undatus

When: Fruits in flushes from summer to autumn.

Tip: Pick when skin colour is vibrant pink or yellow and “flaps” (bracts) are slightly withered but still fresh-looking.

3

Jackfruit

Artocarpus heterophyllus

When: Ripe in summer to autumn, sometimes a second smaller crop.

Tip: Harvest when the fruit develops a strong aroma, dull green-to-yellowing skin, and slight softening with spines flattening.

2

Banana

Musa acuminata

When: Bunches can ripen year-round in warm climates, with most in summer and autumn.

Tip: Pick bananas when the fruit is full-sized and the angles on the fingers have rounded out.

Where to look

The most mapped suburbs right now are Southport (16), Pimpama (7), Miami (7), Varsity Lakes (6), Surfers Paradise (6), Paradise Point (6), Tallebudgera (6) and Currumbin (6). Many more spots are scattered across the rest of the Gold Coast, so check the map for what’s near you.

110 of the edible spots around Gold Coast are on public land such as street berms, parks and reserves. There are also 764 public drinking water taps and fountains on the map, handy for a long walk or ride. And 13 community gardens, many of which welcome visitors and volunteers.

Forage responsibly

  • Ask before picking from trees on private land, even if branches hang over the footpath.
  • Check your local council’s rules for parks and reserves.
  • Take only what you need and leave plenty for others and for wildlife.
  • Only eat what you can identify with certainty, and wash what you pick, especially near busy roads.

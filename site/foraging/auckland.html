What you can pick in Auckland

The most mapped plants around Auckland, with how many spots are on the map, when they’re usually ready and and how to tell they’re ready.

525

Feijoa

Acca sellowiana

When: The main season generally occurs in autumn in temperate regions.

Tip: Pick when fruits drop naturally or come away with a gentle shake of the tree.

272

Lemon

Citrus limon

When: Fruits most of the year, with the main crop in winter to spring.

Tip: A lemon is ripe when it has a bright yellow color, feels firm with a slight give, emits a strong citrus aroma, and is heavy for its size.

196

Rosemary

Salvia rosmarinus

When: Leaves can be picked all year round.

Tip: The edible parts of rosemary include the leaves, flowers, and young tender stems, with leaves being the most commonly used in cooking.

170

Loquat

Eriobotrya japonica

When: Ripe in late winter to spring, one of the first fruits of the year.

Tip: Pick when fruit is fully yellow-orange, fragrant, and slightly soft to the touch.

116

Avocado

Persea americana

When: Fruit matures on the tree for months; depending on the variety it is ready somewhere between late winter and summer.

Tip: Mature fruit should be full-sized and detach easily from the tree.

112

Lime

Citrus × latifolia, C. aurantiifolia

When: Ripe from late summer into winter, with some fruit at other times.

Tip: A lime is ripe when it feels heavy, slightly soft to the touch, has smooth, glossy skin, and emits a fresh, zesty aroma, with colour ranging from vibrant green to slightly yellow depending on the…

100

Orange

Citrus sinensis

When: Navel oranges ripen in winter; Valencia oranges in spring to summer.

Tip: Pick when fruit is fully coloured, heavy, and slightly springy when squeezed.

90

Grapefruit

Citrus paradisi

When: Ripe from winter through spring and holds well on the tree.

Tip: Pick when fruit is fully coloured, heavy, and slightly springy when pressed.

86

Banana

Musa acuminata

When: Bunches can ripen year-round in warm climates, with most in summer and autumn.

Tip: Pick bananas when the fruit is full-sized and the angles on the fingers have rounded out.

75

Mandarin

Citrus reticulata

When: Ripe in late autumn through winter, depending on the variety.

Tip: Pick when fruit is fully orange, fragrant, and slightly soft with a heavy feel.

59

Apple

Malus domestica

When: Ripe from late summer through autumn, depending on the variety and climate.

Tip: Ripe when fruit is fully coloured, comes off easily with a gentle twist, and seeds are brown.

59

Fig

Ficus carica

When: Ripe from late summer into autumn.

Tip: Pick when fruit hangs slightly limp, feels soft, and the skin may show slight splitting or drooping.

Where to look

The most mapped suburbs right now are Mount Albert (215), Point Chevalier (210), Western Springs (149), Morningside (72), Grey Lynn (69), Māngere Bridge (68), Kingsland (63) and Saint Lukes (57). Many more spots are scattered across the rest of Auckland, so check the map for what’s near you.

528 of the edible spots around Auckland are on public land such as street berms, parks and reserves. There are also 637 public drinking water taps and fountains on the map, handy for a long walk or ride. And 46 community gardens, many of which welcome visitors and volunteers.

Forage responsibly

  • Ask before picking from trees on private land, even if branches hang over the footpath.
  • Check your local council’s rules for parks and reserves.
  • Take only what you need and leave plenty for others and for wildlife.
  • Only eat what you can identify with certainty, and wash what you pick, especially near busy roads.

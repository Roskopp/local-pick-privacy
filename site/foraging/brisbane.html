What you can pick in Brisbane

The most mapped plants around Brisbane, with how many spots are on the map, when they’re usually ready and and how to tell they’re ready.

177

Mango

Mangifera indica

When: Ripe from late spring to late summer, starting earlier in the tropics than in subtropical areas.

Tip: Harvest when the fruit reaches full size and starts to show colour change or a slight yellow blush, depending on variety.

40

Macadamia

Macadamia integrifolia

When: Nuts fall mainly from late summer to winter.

Tip: Harvest when nuts drop naturally or husks split open on the tree.

37

Passionfruit

Passiflora edulis

When: Main crop in summer to autumn, often a smaller crop in spring.

Tip: Pick when fruit changes from green to deep purple or yellow and often falls from the vine naturally.

37

Guava

Psidium guajava

When: Ripe from late summer into winter, with some fruit at other times in the tropics.

Tip: Pick when fruit gives slightly to pressure and emits a strong sweet fragrance.

25

Mulberry

Morus alba

When: Ripe from late spring into summer.

Tip: Harvest when berries are fully dark purple-black (or deep red depending on variety) and fall easily with a gentle touch.

20

Papaya

Carica papaya

When: Fruits year-round in warm climates, most heavily in summer and autumn.

Tip: Harvest when skin begins turning yellow-orange and fruit softens slightly at the blossom end.

20

Loquat

Eriobotrya japonica

When: Ripe in late winter to spring, one of the first fruits of the year.

Tip: Pick when fruit is fully yellow-orange, fragrant, and slightly soft to the touch.

14

Grape

Vitis vinifera

When: Ripe from late summer to autumn, depending on the variety.

Tip: Harvest when berries are fully coloured, sweet, and easily detach from the stem.

14

Avocado

Persea americana

When: Fruit matures on the tree for months; depending on the variety it is ready somewhere between late winter and summer.

Tip: Mature fruit should be full-sized and detach easily from the tree.

13

Jackfruit

Artocarpus heterophyllus

When: Ripe in summer to autumn, sometimes a second smaller crop.

Tip: Harvest when the fruit develops a strong aroma, dull green-to-yellowing skin, and slight softening with spines flattening.

12

Banana

Musa acuminata

When: Bunches can ripen year-round in warm climates, with most in summer and autumn.

Tip: Pick bananas when the fruit is full-sized and the angles on the fingers have rounded out.

12

Curry Leaf Tree

Bergera koenigii

When: Leaves can be picked all year in warm climates; growth slows and some leaves drop in cold winters.

Tip: The fresh leaves are an indispensable part of Indian cuisine and Indian traditional medicines.

Where to look

The most mapped suburbs right now are Mango Hill (100), West End (55), Highgate Hill (53), South Brisbane (27), Woolloongabba (26), Dutton Park (18), Kangaroo Point (12) and Moorooka (11). Many more spots are scattered across the rest of Brisbane, so check the map for what’s near you.

145 of the edible spots around Brisbane are on public land such as street berms, parks and reserves. There are also 3,140 public drinking water taps and fountains on the map, handy for a long walk or ride. And 71 community gardens, many of which welcome visitors and volunteers.

Forage responsibly

  • Ask before picking from trees on private land, even if branches hang over the footpath.
  • Check your local council’s rules for parks and reserves.
  • Take only what you need and leave plenty for others and for wildlife.
  • Only eat what you can identify with certainty, and wash what you pick, especially near busy roads.

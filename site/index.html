Questions

What is Local Pick?

Local Pick is a free foraging app with a community map of fruit trees, nut trees, herbs, drinking water and community gardens. Foragers add the spots they find and mark how ripe they are, so you can see what’s worth picking near you right now.

Is Local Pick free?

Yes. Local Pick is free to download and use.

Where does Local Pick work?

Local Pick is available in New Zealand and Australia, from big cities like Auckland, Wellington, Christchurch, Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, Adelaide and Perth to small towns.

Where does the map data come from?

Spots are added and checked by the Local Pick community. The map also includes public open data, such as council street and park tree records and public drinking fountains, matched to the edible plants in the app.

Is foraging legal in New Zealand and Australia?

Rules vary by council and by type of land. Picking a little fruit for yourself from public street trees is often fine, but always check local rules, get permission before picking on private land, and only take what you need. Local Pick shows the land type for each spot to help.

How do I know if something is ripe?

Foragers mark each spot as ripe, almost ripe or not ripe when they visit, and the app shows when it was last updated. You can also favourite a tree and get an alert when someone marks it ripe.

Is it safe to eat what I find?

Only eat plants you can identify with certainty. Wash what you pick, and take extra care near busy roads or anywhere that may have been sprayed.
